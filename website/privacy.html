Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

Last Updated: October 2026 • Universal Clipboard Sync v1.1.0

Summary Guarantee

Universal Clipboard Sync is an open-source productivity utility designed from the ground up to operate locally across your own private Wi-Fi network. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell your personal data or clipboard contents. There are no user accounts, no cloud servers, no usage tracking, and no external advertising networks.

1. What Information We Process

To perform clipboard synchronization as requested by the user, the software processes the following data exclusively between your paired devices:

  • Clipboard Content: Text snippets and images copied by the user are temporarily held in local system memory to be transmitted over your local Wi-Fi connection directly to your paired devices. They are never transmitted to external cloud servers.
  • Device Identifiers: A locally generated UUID (universally unique identifier) and device model name (e.g., "Windows PC", "Tecno Spark 10", "Samsung Galaxy S24") are exchanged during initial QR pairing to establish stable mutual authentication.
  • Cryptographic Pairing Keys: A 256-bit AES master secret is generated locally on your Windows PC and protected at rest using Microsoft Data Protection API (DPAPI). On Android, pairing keys are protected in encrypted system preferences backed by the Android KeyStore.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

Based on our verified source code architecture:

  • We do not operate central user accounts or profile databases.
  • We do not bundle third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Mixpanel).
  • We do not collect crash telemetry or diagnostics without explicit user export.
  • We do not log or store clipboard contents after the application is closed.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

Android Operating System

  • Accessibility Service (ClipboardAccessibilityService): Used solely to detect clipboard copy events when other apps are active so text can sync seamlessly in the background. It is configured with canRetrieveWindowContent="false", meaning it cannot inspect your screen, read passwords, or access other apps' visual data.
  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively to scan the pairing QR code displayed on your PC screen during the 5-second initial pairing process. No camera images or video frames are saved or transmitted.
  • Foreground Service & Notifications: Used to maintain a persistent local WebSocket client and display an actionable "Sync to PC" notification tile.

Windows Operating System

  • Clipboard Access: Listens to Windows clipboard update events and writes received text/images to the Windows clipboard buffer.
  • Local Network Access: Binds a local WebSocket server to port 8765 and a UDP discovery beacon to port 8766 on your local subnet.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Clipboard data is held only in transient memory during transmission and is not written to permanent disk storage. You can instantly delete all pairing credentials by clicking "Unpair All Devices" on Windows, or tapping "Disconnect" on Android.
