Simple Setup

Up and Running in Four Steps

Zero complicated network configurations. Start synchronizing your clipboard in under two minutes.

01

Install on Windows

Download and run the Windows installer (or portable executable). Universal Clipboard starts quietly in your Windows System Tray near the taskbar clock.

02

Display Pairing QR

Click the tray icon to open the Status Window. A secure, ephemeral pairing QR code is generated specifically for your workstation.

Scan from Phone

Open the Android app, tap "Scan QR Code", and point your camera at your computer screen. The cryptographic handshake completes instantly.

Copy & Paste

ANDROID 13, 14 & 15 SECURITY NOTICE

Encountered "Restricted setting" on Android? Here is why and how to enable it.

When you install any application from outside Google Play (such as a downloaded APK from a browser or file manager), Android 13+ automatically marks sensitive settings as restricted to protect users against malicious sideloaded apps. Because Universal Clipboard Sync uses an Accessibility Service to detect when you copy text without opening the app, Android may display:

"Restricted setting. For your security, this setting is currently unavailable."

Step 1: Open App Info

In the Universal Clipboard app, tap "Step 1: App Info (Allow)" (or long-press the app icon on your home screen and tap the ℹ️ App info icon).

Step 2: Allow Restricted Settings

In the top-right corner of the system App Info screen, tap the three vertical dots (⋮) menu. Select "Allow restricted settings" and confirm using your phone PIN or fingerprint.

Step 3: Enable in Accessibility

Return to the app and tap "Step 2: Accessibility" (or go to phone Settings → Accessibility → Universal Clipboard Sync). The switch is now unlocked and can be turned ON!

🔒 Our Privacy Guarantee: The Accessibility Service sets canRetrieveWindowContent="false". It cannot read your screen, cannot inspect passwords, does not store clipboard history, and only communicates with your paired PC over Wi-Fi.

Privacy & Security

Your Data Stays on Your Devices

Universal Clipboard Sync is engineered with zero telemetry, zero analytics tracking, and zero cloud storage.

🔒 Local Network Only

Clipboard content is transferred strictly between your paired devices across your local Wi-Fi router. Content is never routed through third-party servers.

🛡️ AES-256-GCM Encryption

Every text payload and image is encrypted end-to-end using authenticated AES-256-GCM ciphers before transmission over the WebSocket link.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Windows SmartScreen say "Unknown Publisher" or "Windows protected your PC"?

This is standard Windows behavior for newly released and independent open-source software.

  • Why this happens: Microsoft Defender SmartScreen checks for established download volume in Microsoft's telemetry cloud, or an expensive commercial Extended Validation (EV) certificate ($400–$600 USD/year requiring physical hardware security tokens). As a free, community-driven open-source project without a corporate EV subscription, our binaries are digitally signed with BAZIQHUE Authenticode metadata, but have not yet built up thousands of downloads in Microsoft's cloud database.
  • How to proceed (2 quick clicks):
    1. When the blue "Windows protected your PC" dialog appears, click the underlined "More info" text.
    2. Click the "Run anyway" button that appears. The Universal Clipboard setup wizard will launch normally.
  • Independent Hash Verification: You can verify binary integrity anytime by checking the SHA-256 checksum in Windows PowerShell:
    Get-FileHash .\UniversalClipboard-Setup-1.1.0.exe -Algorithm SHA256
    The output will exactly match our published checksum: c94e680a811d127c069ca3816aed2e55a582147de862c7ba5a8b2cbf72c724fd.
  • Direct Publisher Trust (Optional): For power users and system administrators who want Windows to display "Verified publisher: BAZIQHUE" without prompts on their machine, you can download our public certificate (BAZIQHUE-CodeSigning.cer) and import it into your Trusted Publishers store with one PowerShell command:
    Import-Certificate -FilePath .\BAZIQHUE-CodeSigning.cer -CertStoreLocation Cert:\CurrentUser\TrustedPublisher
Does Universal Clipboard require an active internet connection?
No! Universal Clipboard operates entirely over your local Wi-Fi network (or even a mobile phone Wi-Fi hotspot). Even if your internet connection goes down, your devices continue synchronizing without interruption.
How many Android phones can I pair with one Windows computer?
You can pair multiple Android devices simultaneously. The Windows server tracks each phone by a stable unique device identifier. From the desktop window, you can view all paired phones, rename them, or disconnect individual devices.
Why does Android show "Restricted setting" when enabling accessibility?
This is a standard security protection introduced in Android 13+ for apps installed outside the Google Play Store (sideloaded APKs). To allow it: go to phone Settings → Apps → Universal Clipboard → tap the three vertical dots (⋮) in the top-right corner → tap "Allow restricted settings" → authenticate with your screen lock. Then you can turn Accessibility ON.
Is an iPhone (iOS) version available?
Currently, Universal Clipboard Sync supports Windows (10/11) and Android (8.0 to 15). Apple iOS strictly restricts background clipboard monitoring due to sandboxing policies. An iOS companion client with manual sync capabilities is planned for a future release.
What is the difference between the Windows Installer and Portable build?
The Installer registers Universal Clipboard in your Start Menu and creates shortcuts with automatic startup options. The Portable build is a single standalone executable that runs immediately without installing or requiring administrator rights.
How do I unpair or disconnect a device?
On Windows, open the Status Window and click the ❌ icon next to any device to unpair it, or click "Disconnect" to drop an active connection. On Android, tap the "Disconnect" button on the main screen.
